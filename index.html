Image Compressor

Choose an image, adjust quality and format, then download the optimized copy.

Drop an image here or tap to chooseJPG, PNG or WebP • max 15 MB
Processing for this tool happens in your browser. The prototype does not upload your selected image to a server.
Will compression always reduce the file size?

Not always. PNG is lossless, and some already-optimized files may not become smaller. The tool keeps the result only when the browser can produce the requested output.

What quality should I use?

For JPG/WebP, around 65–80% is a practical starting point. Lower values usually produce smaller files but may reduce visual quality. PNG is lossless and can be larger than the original.
