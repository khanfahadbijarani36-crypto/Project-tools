Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy describes the current Project Tools prototype. It should be reviewed and updated before the site is launched publicly with analytics, advertising, forms or other third-party services.

Images and files

The current image tools process selected images in your browser. The prototype does not upload those selected images to a Project Tools server.

Website information

The current prototype does not intentionally collect a name, email address or uploaded image through a Project Tools backend. If third-party services such as analytics, advertising, embedded content or contact-form providers are added later, this policy must be updated to explain them.

Cookies

The current prototype does not intentionally set its own advertising or tracking cookies. This may change when third-party services are added.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided on the Contact page once the project has a public contact address.
